Alfa Romeo workshop questions

What should an Alfa Romeo workshop be able to diagnose?

It should be comfortable with Alfa-compatible diagnostics, battery and low-voltage checks, TCT gearbox values, MultiAir-related symptoms, misfire data and suspension inspection.

Why do Alfa Romeo quotes vary so much?

Parts availability and diagnostic familiarity vary widely. Ask for fault codes, test values and proof of the failed part before approving expensive electrical, gearbox or engine work.
