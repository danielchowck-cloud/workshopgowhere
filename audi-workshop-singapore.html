Audi workshop questions

What diagnostic platform do Audi workshops in Singapore use?

ODIS is the official Audi/Volkswagen factory tool. Many independents also use VCDS, which is a respected community-grade diagnostic platform capable of advanced coding on most VAG cars.

How can I tell if a DSG jerking issue is serious?

DSG/S tronic shifting concerns often resolve with a mechatronic adaptation, software update or fluid service before any internal work. Ask for ODIS or VCDS log evidence before approving an overhaul.
