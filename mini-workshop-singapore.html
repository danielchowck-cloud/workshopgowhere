MINI workshop questions

Can BMW specialists work on MINI?

Many can, because MINI is BMW-group and ISTA diagnostics are useful. Still ask whether the shop has experience with your MINI generation and engine family.

What are common MINI repair quote traps?

Mount vibration, coolant leaks and ALL4 shudder can be over-quoted as gearbox or engine failure. Ask for lifted inspection, pressure-test results and ISTA scan data.
