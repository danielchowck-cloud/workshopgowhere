Aircon not cold: do you really need compressor replacement?

2026-05-06 · 4 min read

Weak aircon is common in Singapore because the system works hard every day. But weak cooling does not always mean compressor replacement.

Owner takeaway

Do not approve a major repair from a vague symptom alone. Ask what test confirmed the failed part, then compare itemised quotes.

Common cheaper causes

Low refrigerant, small leaks, clogged condenser, expansion valve issues, dirty cabin filter or fan problems can all cause weak cooling.

What proof to ask for

Ask for pressure readings, leak test, temperature reading at vent and why compressor is confirmed failed.

Red flag quote

If the workshop says “compressor weak” without measurements, ask them to show the readings.

When compressor makes sense

Noisy compressor, poor pressure behaviour or confirmed internal failure can justify replacement.
