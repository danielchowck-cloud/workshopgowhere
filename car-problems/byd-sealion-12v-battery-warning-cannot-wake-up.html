BYD Sealion: 12V battery warning / car does not wake up

If you are seeing this symptom in Singapore, use this as a pre-workshop checklist. The goal is to identify the likely root cause, avoid replacing the wrong part, and know the fair repair range before approving work.

Likely fix
12V auxiliary system test, battery replacement, DC-DC charging check

What this usually means

Even on new EVs, many apparent dead-car problems start with the 12V auxiliary system. Check 12V voltage, DC-DC charging behaviour, and fault history before assuming high-voltage battery failure.

What to ask the workshop before approving repair

  • • Show the diagnostic fault codes.
  • • Show photo/video proof of the failed part.
  • • Give an itemised quote for parts and labour.
  • • Explain why cheaper common fixes were ruled out.
  • • Use/confirm: EV diagnostic scanner
  • • Use/confirm: 12V battery load tester

This is not a final diagnosis. It is a money-saving checklist before you go workshop.
