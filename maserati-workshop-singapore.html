Maserati workshop questions

Why are Maserati repair quotes so high?

Parts cost and limited specialist familiarity can push quotes up quickly. Ask for diagnostic proof and consider a second opinion before approving suspension, electrical or engine-leak work.

What should a Maserati specialist be able to show?

They should show fault codes, battery/sleep-current data for electrical issues, suspension scan data, and photos or pressure-test proof for coolant and oil leaks.
