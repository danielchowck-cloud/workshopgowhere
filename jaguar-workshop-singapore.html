Jaguar workshop questions

Do Jaguar workshops use the same tools as Land Rover?

Often yes. Jaguar and Land Rover share JLR diagnostic platforms, so JLR SDD/Pathfinder capability matters for XE, XF, F-Pace, E-Pace and F-Type faults.

What should I ask before approving a Jaguar gearbox quote?

Ask for JLR fault codes, fluid/service history, adaptation values and mount inspection before approving a full gearbox replacement.
